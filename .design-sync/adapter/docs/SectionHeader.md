Section header: glowing "// eyebrow" + line icon, uppercase h2 with a neon underline bar, optional intro and MoreLink. `tone="trail"` switches eyebrow/bar/glow to warn-red (recovery, hazard, events).

```jsx
<SectionHeader
  eyebrow="// Air Down · Air Up"
  iconName="offroad"
  title="Offroad Essentials"
  intro="Tire management is everything off-road."
>
  <MoreLink href="/roundups/tire-inflators">Roundup: best tire inflators &rarr;</MoreLink>
</SectionHeader>
```
