Masonry photo columns (2 → 3 → 4 across breakpoints) of GalleryItems.

```jsx
<GalleryGrid>
  <GalleryItem src="/photos/trail-1.jpg" alt="Forest trail run" width={1200} height={900} />
  <GalleryItem src="/photos/trail-2.jpg" alt="Mountain terrain" width={1200} height={1600} />
</GalleryGrid>
```
