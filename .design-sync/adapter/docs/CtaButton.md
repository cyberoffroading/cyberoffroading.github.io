The glowing angular CTA. Primary = filled cyan gradient (ONE per card — glow is earned). Secondary = red outline neon for the alternative pick. External links auto-append ↗ and open in a new tab; `internal` suppresses both for in-page nav.

```jsx
<CtaButton href="https://amzn.to/xyz">Check Price on Amazon</CtaButton>
<CtaButton variant="secondary" href="https://amzn.to/abc">MKII — Budget Option</CtaButton>
<CtaButton internal href="#winch">Explore Gear &rarr;</CtaButton>
```
